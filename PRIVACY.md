# Privacy

BluntyBuds does not collect, send, or store any data off your device.

- The Android app has no internet permission. It asks only for Bluetooth (Nearby devices) and, optionally, notifications.
- The Windows app makes no network connections.
- No accounts, analytics, crash reporting, ads, or automatic updates.
- Settings (last device, EQ presets, options) are stored locally on your device only.
