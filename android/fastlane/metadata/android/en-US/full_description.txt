BluntyBuds is a free, open-source, offline earbuds companion. It talks to the earbuds directly over Bluetooth Low Energy.

<b>No internet. No account. No tracking.</b>
<ul>
<li>No INTERNET permission. On Android 10 and 11 it asks for location, which Android requires for Bluetooth scanning</li>
<li>No account, no analytics, no ads, no auto-update, no proprietary libraries</li>
<li>About and Privacy lists only the permissions you granted</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Home on one screen: device, connection dot, L / Case / R battery pills (number plus bar), Offline badge</li>
<li>Solo Mode: with one earbud in use, the missing bud's pill fades to an outline instead of 0%</li>
<li>Single-earbud battery: the notification and low-battery alerts use only the earbud in use</li>
<li>Noise: Quiet, Aware, Off, Custom with a Depth slider (where supported)</li>
<li>Tune: 10-band EQ (±12 dB), sent as you change it, with a Lock EQ padlock</li>
<li>Sound Profiles: a dropdown that applies on selection with a 3-second Undo; Edit to save, rename or delete; import/export</li>
<li>AutoEQ: built-in correction profiles from AutoEq measurements; profiles never boost</li>
<li>Import Correction: any ParametricEQ.txt or GraphicEQ.txt, fitted to the 10 bands offline</li>
<li>Gestures: one dropdown per gesture and side, only for gestures your earbuds report</li>
<li>L-R Balance, Low-Latency, Ping Buds</li>
<li>About and Privacy, Quick Settings noise tile, optional status notification, auto-reconnect</li>
</ul>

Unofficial companion. See the project's README for the list of compatible earbuds; other models are detected and marked "untested".

AutoEQ profiles: AutoEq by Jaakko Pasanen (MIT).

A Windows version is available from the project's GitHub page.
