BluntyBuds is a free, open-source, unofficial controller for TOZO Bluetooth earbuds. It talks to the earbuds directly over Bluetooth Low Energy and works completely offline.

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>No INTERNET permission</li>
<li>No account, no analytics, no ads, no auto-update</li>
<li>No proprietary libraries</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Battery level for each earbud</li>
<li>Noise control: off, noise cancelling, ambient, custom depth; game mode</li>
<li>10-band equalizer (±12 dB) with presets, import/export</li>
<li>AutoEQ: built-in correction profiles (NC2, NC7, NC9, T6, T10, T12) from AutoEq measurements; the connected model's profile is listed first</li>
<li>Import any AutoEQ ParametricEQ.txt or GraphicEQ.txt, fitted offline to the 10 bands</li>
<li>Configurable touch controls and balance</li>
<li>Find my earbuds (play a chirp)</li>
<li>Quick Settings noise tile, optional status notification with noise buttons, low-battery alert, auto-reconnect</li>
</ul>

Tested on TOZO NC9 and A1; other models are detected and marked "untested".

BluntyBuds is an unofficial project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TOZO. "TOZO" is used only to describe compatibility.

AutoEQ profiles: AutoEq by Jaakko Pasanen (MIT).

A Windows version is available from the project's GitHub page.
